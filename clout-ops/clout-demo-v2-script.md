# Clout demo film, v2, concise cut with motion graphics

Voice: British female, twenties. en-GB-LibbyNeural (Edge neural, rendered locally, no key).
Runtime target: about three minutes. Narration about 470 words, graphics carry the explanation,
footage proves it is real.

Palette (taken from the product's own UI so the graphics match the film):
bg `#0D0D0F`, panel `#141416`, ink `#F5F5F3`, muted `#8A8A8E`,
accent `#5C8FB8`, accent light `#8FB4D4`, positive `#30D158`, warn `#F4C95D`.
Easing `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` everywhere. Entrances 0.45-0.55s,
micro moves 0.12-0.18s, section moves 0.65s. Never pop in. Never hard cut between beats:
wipe or morph, or let the outgoing element leave frame as the next one enters.

---

## Beat 1, hook (footage, landing page)

> For a century, attention was free. You made it, the platform sold it, and you kept a follower
> count. Clout is a bet that this was never the deal anyone wanted.

Visual: the real landing page, slow push. Words land as an overlay: "attention" then "value".

## Beat 2, what it is (graphic, kinetic type)

> Clout is the social layer for Arc, an EVM chain built for payments. Posts become markets,
> profiles become records, and the value you create stays with you.

Graphic: the word CLOUT sets in from a wide tracking, then three lines rise in sequence,
each swapping one word: "posts become markets", "profiles become records", "value stays with you".

## Beat 3, the broken loop (graphic, diagram)

> The old loop is simple and it is broken. You create the attention. The platform sells the
> attention. You receive a follower count and a promise. Reach is rented. Reputation sits in a
> database you do not control. Nothing you build follows you out the door.

Graphic: a three node ring, YOU to PLATFORM to REVENUE, drawn as a line that completes, then the
line breaks at YOU and the node dims. The ring then re-forms as YOU to POST to MARKET with the
payout arrow returning to YOU, in accent, and settles.

## Beat 4, identity (footage, connect and profile)

> On Clout your wallet is your account. No email, no password, nobody holding your identity.
> Connect once, and the posts, the follows and the history attach to an address you own. Humans
> can verify who they are, and the feed labels automated accounts honestly instead of hiding them.

Visual: the connect flow, then the profile. Callout chips point at the address, the verification
mark, and the automated-account label.

## Beat 5, the feed (footage, feed and tabs)

> Discover shows what is working right now. Following is the people you chose, in order. Latest is
> raw time. Arc settles in about four hundred milliseconds and pays gas in USDC, so money moves
> inside the feed without a wallet popup interrupting what you were reading.

Visual: the three scope tabs, a slow scroll. Callout near the composer: "gas in USDC".

## Beat 6, the market (graphic, mechanism)

> Here is the part that changes behaviour. Every post has shares, with a visible price, a visible
> supply, and a visible curve. Believe a post early and you buy it. If everyone else arrives later,
> the price moves and you were right in public. The author takes five percent of every trade, the
> protocol takes five percent to keep the market alive, and every payment is a pull payment, so one
> broken wallet cannot freeze the market for everybody else.

Graphic: a post card at centre. A share strip builds under it: supply counter climbing, price line
drawing upward, curve drawn as a shallow arc. Then a fee split bar divides 5 / 5 / 90 with the two
five percent slices labelled and the remainder returning to the trader.

## Beat 7, trust (footage, trade sheet, gated rollout)

> Money features do not get to be rushed. The trade sheet shows supply, price, your position and the
> activity around the post. Until the contracts are audited the interface says exactly that. Trust is
> not a disclaimer at the bottom of a screen. Trust is the decision not to pretend something is
> finished when it is not.

Visual: open a real post's trade sheet, hold on the numbers. Callout: "read only, pre audit".
Then the staged rollout notice.

## Beat 8, creator keys (graphic, mechanism, short)

> The same idea extends from a post to a person. Creator keys let a community back the people whose
> work they want more of, with the curve and the fees visible on chain.

Graphic: the post-market arc morphs into a person node with a key ring; supply and fee labels
reuse the same positions from beat 6 so the viewer recognises the mechanism.

## Beat 9, effortless (footage, search, composer, portrait)

> And it has to feel effortless. Search people by name, handle or wallet. Find posts by what they
> say. Navigation stays where your thumb expects it, and writing starts in one motion. No maze, no
> manual, no ceremony.

Visual: search field typed into, then the composer, then the portrait phone frame.

## Beat 10, close (footage into title card)

> Attention is the most valuable thing we produce, and today we give it away for nothing. Clout
> gives it rules. Clout, the social layer for Arc.

Visual: the feed, dimmed, then the title card: CLOUT, the social layer for Arc, cloutonarc.fun.
